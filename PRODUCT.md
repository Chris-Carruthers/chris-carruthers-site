## Platform

## Stack

## Users

A personal site for Chris Carruthers, read by four audiences (all confirmed):
- Investors and prospective co-founders sizing him up as a founder.
- Boards and executive recruiters evaluating him for CEO / CPO / CTO-scope roles.
- Customers and partners (health systems, health-tech partners).
- Builders he would hire: engineers, PMs, designers.

They arrive from LinkedIn, an intro, or a search, usually spending a few minutes, often on a phone, deciding whether to take a meeting.

## Product Purpose

Prove, with evidence, that Chris can see where a market is going, build the product himself, build the team and platform around it, and commit through to customers going live. Success: the visitor reaches LinkedIn or GitHub convinced, with specifics to repeat.

## Positioning

A founder-operator who works across all three executive seats: company (founding team of a Providence spin-out, plus his own earlier companies), product (led product org and strategy), and technology (personally the top committer on the AI product he leads, and author of the platform architecture). Few executives ship ~2,000 commits in a year while also writing the product strategy and the platform PRD.
